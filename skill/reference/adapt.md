# Adapt

Adapt the requested experience to its target device, input, viewport, or delivery context.

Identify source and target contexts and the primary task that must survive.
Use suitable responsive, native, print, or email knowledge for the actual target.
Preserve content, reachable capability, identity, semantics, and source order;
adaptation may require restructuring rather than shrinking the layout.

## Verify Adaptations

Check relevant sizes, orientations, long content, zoom, keyboard, and support
policy. For custom sliders, drag surfaces, and scrollable control strips:

- **Primary gesture**: tap and complete the drag using the target input method.
- **Scroll across it**: scroll across the control without activating it; drag
  along its axis without incorrectly scrolling the page.
- **Evidence**: say what produced the evidence: emulated viewport, synthesized
  touch and its browser engine, or physical device. Screenshots and resized
  viewports verify layout, never a gesture. Unavailable hardware is a reported
  gap, not a blocker; name what stayed untested.

## Completion

Verify the corrected behavior and report remaining context or input limits.
Do not add a separate polish workflow unless the requested outcome needs it.

Apply [craft-floor.md](craft-floor.md) before implementation. Use the resolved
target and existing answers; ask only about material gaps through the host's
structured question tool when available. These contracts follow
the host’s authorization, questions, delegation, and verification budget.
